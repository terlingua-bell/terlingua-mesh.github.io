How it works

Your message hops ridge to ridge.

MeshCore is open-source firmware for small, low-power LoRa radios. Your phone talks to your radio over Bluetooth. Your radio talks to ours — solar repeaters we keep up on high ground — and they pass your message along until it reaches your neighbor. No SIM card, no Wi-Fi, no internet anywhere in the chain.

01 — YOUR RADIO

A pocket-size node

A small LoRa radio — many cost less than a tank of gas — paired with the free MeshCore app on your phone or computer.

02 — OUR REPEATERS

Up on the high ground

Terlingua Mesh installs and maintains solar-powered repeaters with a clear view across the ranch, so your signal carries well past your own ridge.

03 — THE MESH

Everyone helps everyone

Every repeater added by a neighbor makes the network reach farther and hold up better. Nobody owns it, and it doesn't shut off when the grid does.

Get on the mesh

Connected in an afternoon.

You'll need one small radio and a phone. If any of this sounds like too much, bring your radio to a clinic and we'll do it with you.

  1. Get a LoRa radio

    Pick any MeshCore-compatible device made for the US 915 MHz band. Ask us before you buy and we'll point you at what's working well on the ranch.

    HeltecLilyGORAK WisBlockSeeed SenseCAP

  2. Flash Companion firmware

    Plug the radio into a computer and load the MeshCore Companion firmware using the web flasher at flasher.meshcore.co.uk. It takes about two minutes in Chrome or Edge.

  3. Install the MeshCore app

    Free for iPhone, Android, and desktop. Pair it with your radio over Bluetooth and set a display name your neighbors will recognize.

  4. Match our radio settings

    Set your radio to the Terlingua Mesh network settings shown here. Frequency, bandwidth, and spreading factor have to match exactly or you won't hear anyone.

  5. Send an advert & say howdy

    Tap the advert button so others can find you, then post a hello on the public channel. Welcome to the mesh.

Questions

Asked around the ranch.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no plan, no subscription and no account. Your only cost is the radio you buy once. Terlingua Mesh maintains the shared repeaters as a service to the community.

Do I need a ham radio license?

No. MeshCore radios in the US operate in the unlicensed 902–928 MHz ISM band using low power, so anyone can use them.

Is it the internet? Can I text a regular phone number?

No — and that's the point. The mesh only connects MeshCore radios to each other. It can't reach regular phone numbers or websites, which is exactly why it keeps working when those are down.

Who can read my messages?

Direct messages are encrypted between you and the person you're writing to. Messages on the public channel can be read by anyone who has the channel key, so treat it like a bulletin board.

How far will my radio reach?

On its own, a handheld radio might reach a few miles depending on terrain. Through our repeaters on high ground, your message can travel much farther across the ranch. Getting your antenna up high and clear of obstacles makes a big difference.

How's the battery life?

LoRa radios sip power. Many companion radios run for days on a charge, and they charge off any USB port, power bank, or small solar panel.

Is this the same as Meshtastic?

They're both LoRa mesh projects, but they're different systems and don't talk to each other. Our network runs MeshCore, so make sure your radio has MeshCore firmware.
